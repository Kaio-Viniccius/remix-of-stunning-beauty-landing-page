## Application rules
- Keep the clinic landing page on the index route with route-level metadata, because this is the primary visitor experience.
- Define editorial styling and motion in the global semantic design system and expose CTA treatments through Button variants, so presentation stays consistent.
- Keep clinic content static and appointment actions as external WhatsApp links; no backend is needed for this informational page.
- Use generated editorial imagery as illustrative photography only, never as a claimed portrait of the practitioner or her clinic.
